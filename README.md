# Awesome AI Study Guides [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of AI tools and resources for turning course notes, lectures, and readings into study guides, revision handbooks, flashcards, and practice questions.

A good study guide is the course compressed into something you can actually revise from. AI tools can now draft a full handbook from a syllabus or outline, pull flashcards out of lecture notes, and summarize the papers on a reading list. This list covers generators, note-taking, spaced repetition, reading and research helpers, mind maps, and tools for exporting a finished guide to print or e-reader. AI output can be wrong: check facts, formulas, and citations against your course material before you study from it or share it with classmates, and follow your school's rules on AI use.

⭐ = recommended pick.

## Study Guide and Handbook Generators

Tools that write a study guide, summary, or revision material from your topic or your own sources.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Writes a complete study guide or exam revision handbook (about 5,000 to 120,000 words) from a topic, a brief, or an uploaded outline, with chapters, a title, and a cover; export to Word, EPUB, or Markdown to read on an e-reader or print. Text only. $0 7-day trial (card required) includes a first 10,000-word book, then $24.99/month.
- ⭐ [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com/) - Google notebook that answers questions grounded in the sources you upload and can generate study guides, FAQs, and audio overviews.
- [Coconote](https://coconote.app/) - Records or imports lectures and turns them into notes, flashcards, and quizzes.
- [Knowt](https://knowt.com/) - Notes and flashcards with AI-generated quizzes and study guides from your class material.
- [Mindgrasp](https://www.mindgrasp.ai/) - AI study assistant that turns lectures, PDFs, and videos into notes, summaries, flashcards, and quizzes.
- [Revisely](https://www.revisely.com/) - AI generator for flashcards and quizzes from your notes and documents.
- [StudyFetch](https://www.studyfetch.com/) - Turns uploaded course materials into flashcards, quizzes, and notes with an AI tutor.

## Notes and Lecture Capture

Where the raw material for a study guide comes from.

- ⭐ [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) - Local Markdown notes with links between ideas, easy to turn into an outline for a guide.
- [Joplin](https://joplinapp.org/) - Open-source note-taking app with Markdown and end-to-end encrypted sync.
- [Logseq](https://logseq.com/) - Open-source outliner for daily notes and linked topics.
- [Notion](https://www.notion.com/) - Workspace for notes, databases, and course planners.
- [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai/) - Live transcription and summaries for lectures and meetings.

## Flashcards and Spaced Repetition

Turn the guide into something you can drill.

- ⭐ [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) - Open-source spaced repetition flashcards with a large add-on ecosystem.
- [Brainscape](https://www.brainscape.com/) - Flashcards with confidence-based repetition.
- [GoConqr](https://www.goconqr.com/) - Mind maps, flashcards, notes, and quizzes in one study platform.
- [Mochi](https://mochi.cards/) - Markdown flashcards with spaced repetition.
- [Quizlet](https://quizlet.com/) - Widely used flashcard and study set platform.
- [RemNote](https://www.remnote.com/) - Notes and spaced repetition flashcards in the same document.

## Reading and Research Helpers

For courses with long reading lists.

- ⭐ [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/) - Free, open-source reference manager for collecting, annotating, and citing sources.
- [Consensus](https://consensus.app/) - AI search engine that answers questions from peer-reviewed research.
- [Elicit](https://elicit.com/) - AI research assistant for finding, summarizing, and extracting data from papers.
- [Hypothesis](https://hypothes.is/) - Open annotation for web pages and PDFs, alone or with a study group.
- [MarginNote](https://www.marginnote.com/) - PDF and e-book reader that links highlights to mind maps and flashcards.
- [Readwise](https://www.readwise.io/) - Collects highlights from books and articles and resurfaces them for review.
- [Scholarcy](https://www.scholarcy.com/) - Summarizes research papers into key points and flashcards.

## Mind Maps and Outlines

Plan the structure of a guide before writing or generating it.

- ⭐ [Markmap](https://markmap.js.org/) - Open-source tool that turns a Markdown outline into an interactive mind map.
- [MindMeister](https://www.mindmeister.com/) - Collaborative online mind mapping.
- [Whimsical](https://whimsical.com/) - Mind maps, flowcharts, and docs on one canvas.
- [Xmind](https://xmind.com/) - Mind mapping and brainstorming app for desktop and mobile.

## Tutors and Practice

- [Khanmigo](https://www.khanmigo.ai/) - Khan Academy's AI tutor that guides students through problems instead of handing out answers.
- [Wolfram|Alpha](https://www.wolframalpha.com/) - Computational engine with step-by-step solutions for math and science practice.

## Formatting and Export

Turn notes or a generated draft into a clean handout, PDF, or e-book.

- ⭐ [Pandoc](https://pandoc.org/) - Universal document converter between Markdown, Word, EPUB, PDF, and more.
- [Book Page Count Estimator](https://grades2018.github.io/book-page-count-estimator/) - Free browser tool that estimates printed page count from a word count by trim size (by this list's curator).
- [calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/) - E-book manager and converter for sending guides to an e-reader.
- [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/) - Online LaTeX editor, handy for formula-heavy guides.
- [Quarto](https://quarto.org/) - Open-source publishing system for documents and books from Markdown.
- [Typst](https://typst.app/) - Modern typesetting system and web editor, a lighter alternative to LaTeX.
